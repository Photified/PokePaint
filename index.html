How to play

  1. Choose colours from memory. The original stays hidden until grading.
  2. Use Bucket fill for a section or Brush for finer details. Both stay inside the Pokémon.
  3. Tap Grade my painting to compare. Unpainted areas score zero, including white areas. Paint those white too.
  4. Earn A at 80%+ or A+ at 95%+. Every consecutive A or A+ adds to your streak.

Close shades receive partial credit. The score compares painted colours with the original pixel art, with a small allowance for shading. Dark outlines and the background are excluded.
